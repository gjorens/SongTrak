Getting started with SongTrak



1. View a song
Tap a song in the list to see its chords above the lyrics. Use the Key control to transpose (the number of half-tones is shown) and the capo − / + buttons to change the capo. Your key and capo are saved separately from the song's Original Key. When you change either, a bar appears so you can Save or Discard.


2. Add a song
Tap Add song, type or paste a chord sheet, and fill in the title and artist. Chords go in square brackets before the word, like [G]Hello, and section names start with #, like # Verse. Use Detect key next to Original Key, then add Style, Mood and Keywords. Keywords you have used before appear in a drop-down.


3. Key, capo and chords
Original Key is the key of the recording. My Key is the key you want to sound in. Pick it and the app works out the capo for you, or rewrites the chords if you choose that instead. Original Key is never changed by My Key.


4. Find songs
Search by title, artist or keyword. Sort by title, artist, date added and more. A leading "The" is ignored when sorting. The Songs ▾ menu switches between All Songs and any setlist.


5. Setlists
Add a song to a setlist from the drop-down in the song view. Open Setlists to create, rename, delete and reorder them. When a setlist is showing, the sort list has Set order. Use ⏮ and ⏭ to step through the set. Deleting a setlist can also delete its songs.


6. Play and drums
Use Play mode on stage for a larger, uncluttered view. Syncing never interrupts a song while you are playing. Tap 🥁 Drums in the Play bar for a backing beat. Choose a pattern, set the tempo (or tap it), and add swing or a human feel. Tick Start and stop with scroll to run the drums with auto-scroll. Open Edit the beat to change any hit. Choose 1 to 4 Bars for a longer pattern. Turnaround plays a fill instead of the last bar every 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 12 or 16 bars; the fill rotates through three different fills (Fill 1, 2 and 3), and you can change each on its tab. Your fills are kept even if you turn the Turnaround off, and Save fills as keeps the set under My fills for any song.  and tap More sounds for crash, ride, toms, clap, cowbell, shaker and tambourine. Save as my pattern keeps a beat under a name, and it then appears under My patterns in the Pattern list for any song. Each song remembers its own drum settings and shares them with the song file.


7. Listen and find chords
♫ Listen & find ▾ opens a search for the song in Spotify, YouTube Music, Apple Music or YouTube, or in Ultimate Guitar, Chordie, E-Chords, Songsterr or Google to find chords and tabs. Copy a chart you find, then use Import → From clipboard.


Tuner and tunings
🎸 Tune opens a guitar tuner. Tap Start listening, allow the microphone, and pluck one string. The needle shows how far off you are and turns green when you are in tune. Tap a string to hear its note. Choose a tuning such as Drop D or DADGAD, or enter your own notes. Edit a song to set its Tuning, and it shows next to the key. A song in a different tuning is marked in the song list.


8. Share
Share opens the iPad Share sheet, where you can email, message, WhatsApp, print or tap Save to Files. The song is always sent with its key, capo and details, so another SongTrak user gets everything. On a computer, Share ▾ shows a menu instead. Setlists share the same way, with every song included in one file. If an imported song has the same name as one you already have, you can keep both.


9. Import
Import accepts text from the clipboard, one or more files, or a whole folder. It reads ChordPro (.pro, .cho, .crd, .chopro), .onsong and .txt files, SongTrak files, and OnSong backup files (.backup). A zip of text files imports as one song per file, and a text file with {new_song} lines between songs is split into songs. When you import several songs, the app asks you to confirm first.


Full screen on iPad
The ⛶ button at the top switches to full screen when your browser allows it. For the best result, open SongTrak in Safari, tap Share, choose Add to Home Screen, and launch it from that icon. It then runs full screen with no browser bars.


Backing up your songs
Your songs are kept inside the app on this device. Tap Backup and restore, then Save backup file, now and then to keep a safe copy. On an iPad or iPhone choose a folder such as iCloud Drive when the list appears. To move your songs to another device, open that backup file there with Open backup file. A yellow bar at the top reminds you when you have changes that are not backed up yet.


10. Settings and sync
The ⚙ button sets text size, colours, sharps or flats, and light or dark appearance. These apply to the whole app on this device. Cloud sync keeps your library up to date across devices, and if two devices change the same thing, the latest change wins.


11. Delete, select and undo
In the Songs list, swipe a song left to show a red Delete button, tap it and confirm. A black Undo bar appears at the bottom of the screen for 30 seconds. Tap Select (beside Current) to tick several songs, then Delete them together or Export them as ChordPro text, as a zip or as one file, to share by email, AirDrop or Files. Deleted songs are also kept: open Backup and restore, then Recover songs, then Backups… to restore one.


12. History and Recover songs
The ‹ button at the top left lists the songs and setlists you have viewed. Tap one to go back to it. Recover songs (in Backup and restore) keeps a log of repairs, up to three automatic backups of your library, and any damaged copy that could not be read, so nothing is lost silently.


Tips
Edit a song to fix chords at any time. Save your library as a file now and then as a backup.

Loops

Foot pedal and keyboard




Rec replaces a track. Dub adds a new layer on top of what is already recorded. Undo steps back one recording. All tracks stay in step because they share one loop length. If the loops sound late or early, adjust Latency.
