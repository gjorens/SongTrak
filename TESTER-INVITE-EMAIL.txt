STEP 1: Open the link
On an iPad or iPhone, open the link in Safari (the blue compass icon). It needs to be Safari, not another browser. On an Android phone or tablet, use Chrome. On a computer, any browser is fine.

STEP 2: Put it on your home screen (optional, but it makes it feel like a real app)
- iPad or iPhone: tap the Share button (a square with an arrow pointing up), scroll down, tap "Add to Home Screen", then tap "Add".
- Android: tap the three dots at the top right, then "Install app" or "Add to Home screen".
After that, open SongTrak from its icon.

STEP 3: Look around
- Tap the "?" button at the top left. It opens a short guide, with numbered sections you can scroll through.
- Tap any song in the list to see its chords and lyrics.
- Tap "Add song" and try pasting in a song with its chords. Most chord sites let you copy the text.

STEP 4: Try Play mode
- Open a song and tap "Play". The screen gets larger and easier to read on a music stand.
- Tap "Start scroll" and the song scrolls by itself. The "+" and "-" next to Speed change how fast.
- Tap "Start" next to the drum icon for a backing beat. Turn the volume up first. On an iPad, make sure the silent switch is off.
